ABOUT PRAVAH

Where Innovation Meets Creativity

PRAVAH is the annual Technical, Cultural and Sports Festival that brings together thousands of students from across the country. It is a celebration of innovation, creativity, competition and unforgettable memories.

From coding competitions and robotics to dance battles, music concerts and thrilling sports events, Pravah offers something for everyone.

+
